Why this night matters

A lot of work, finally on the big screen.

Scriptfest is about giving Northern Ontario filmmakers a real path from a pitch to a finished film. Seeing three of those films at Cinéfest on the same night — alongside a feature I shot — is a pretty special full-circle moment.
